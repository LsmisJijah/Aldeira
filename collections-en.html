Collections

Editions made to last

Al Muwashsha is AL DEIRA’s first edition — an heirloom object composed of selected walnut, natural mother-of-pearl inlay, refined metal detailing, and silk.

It was conceived as more than a gift box: a piece that remains after the occasion, carrying a trace of Damascus in its material and making.

The collection is offered in two distinct expressions.

The Al Muwashsha Collection

Two editions, two expressions

Royal Edition

Emerald

Dark walnut and natural mother-of-pearl inlay frame a deep emerald interior. A more formal expression of the collection, with details that reveal themselves the closer you come.

Discover the Edition →
Turquoise Edition

Turquoise

The Turquoise edition is lighter and more luminous, drawing on the clarity of turquoise and mother-of-pearl while retaining the quiet character of Al Muwashsha.
